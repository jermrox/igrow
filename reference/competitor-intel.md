# iGrow Competitor & Adjacent-Landscape Intel

Reference dataset for the iGrow outreach pipeline. Maintained for two pipeline consumers:

- **Lead Scraper & Daily Updater** — uses these companies as a named lead source. The
  people who follow, engage with, or work at these companies (especially their *users* and
  *customers*) are high-intent iGrow prospects. Scrape their LinkedIn followers, post-engagers,
  event attendees, and review-site reviewers, then run them through the normal Fit Score + routing.
- **Email Personalization** — when a prospect already uses one of these tools, the email can
  differentiate iGrow directly (e.g. "unlike a coach-matching platform or a call recorder, iGrow
  makes you rehearse the actual conversation out loud and scores the substance of what you said").

Categories mirror iGrow's two competitive fronts: the **development/coaching/L&D** front and the
**AI roleplay/speech/interview/sales** front. LinkedIn slugs were verified via web search.
Note: **Yoodli** appears on both lists — it straddles AI speech coaching and crucial-conversation roleplay.
