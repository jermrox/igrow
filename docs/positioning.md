# iGrow — Positioning & Reference

## What iGrow is
"The flight simulator for the conversations that decide your career." Users rehearse
high-stakes conversations **out loud** with an AI that pushes back, then get a scored report
that grades the **substance** of what they said — not delivery — using the ANCHOR scoring
framework. Records are portable and owned by the individual.

## Beachhead users
- First-time managers in their first 90 days.
- Active job-seekers with an offer in play (negotiation stage).

## Core use cases
- Mock interviews.
- Salary negotiation rehearsal.
- Difficult manager conversations (PIPs, firings, feedback).
- Performance review prep.

## Unit economics (reference)

## Anti-spam rules (all email agents must obey)
1. No ALL CAPS. 2. No urgency language. 3. No misleading subjects. 4. Under 200 words.
5. Conversational tone. 6. Single CTA. 7. Research-first personalization (name the specific
program/firm/role). 8. No attachments on first contact. 9. One hyperlink max initially.
10. No exclamation marks in subject lines. 11. No fake personalization tokens.
12. No "revolutionary/game-changing" language. 13. Subject under 60 chars. 14. Plain text preferred.
